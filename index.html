Alejandro Medina Valenzuela

I am a development economist with specialization in data analytics. I have experience supporting governments, local and international organization such as UNICEF, SNV, and the World Bank on climate-resilient costing, financial planning, climate finance, and evidence-based policymaking across Asia-Pacific and Latin America.

My work combines lifecycle costing, public expenditure, economic evaluation, spatial analysis, and quantitative modelling to inform adaptation planning and strengthen resilience outcomes in developing countries. I have led and contributed to projects on climate-resilient sanitation, public finance tracking, greenhouse gas emissions, WASH financing, and climate adaptation planning.

Moreover, I have presented my research at international conferences such as the CRP–Water Security and WASH in Asia‑Pacific Conference, IWA‑ASPIRE, and the WASH Economics Conference.
